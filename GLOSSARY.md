# Elder Care App v2

Coordination of assistance by trusted people to support older adults' daily lives, dignity, autonomy, and independence.

## Language

**Older adult**:
A person receiving coordinated assistance with aspects of daily life, whether or not they personally use the application. Receiving assistance does not itself imply reduced decision-making authority.

**Trusted individual**:
A family member, friend, or other trusted person involved in supporting an older adult. Coordinating assistance does not itself confer legal decision-making authority.

**Care relationship**:
A person's involvement in supporting a particular older adult, with responsibilities and access considered in that specific relationship. A person may have multiple care relationships and may also receive assistance themselves.

**Informal helper**:
A family member, friend, neighbor, or other trusted person providing assistance in a personal capacity.

**Individual professional**:
A paid caregiver or professional service provider participating in assistance in their individual capacity, distinct from an organization managing staff.

**Organization**:
An entity, such as a home care agency, that employs or coordinates multiple caregivers.

**Need**:
An identified requirement for assistance in an older adult's daily life. Identification alone does not mean anyone has accepted responsibility or that assistance has occurred.

**Coordination responsibility**:
An explicitly accepted responsibility to arrange for a particular need to be addressed, distinct from personally providing the assistance.

**Assistance responsibility**:
An explicitly accepted commitment to provide assistance for a particular need. The same person may accept both coordination and assistance responsibilities.

**Completion report**:
An attributable statement that a specific assistance responsibility has been fulfilled. The report alone is not independent verification of the outcome or evidence that related responsibilities were fulfilled.
